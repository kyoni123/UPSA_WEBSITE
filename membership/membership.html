Membership is required.

A current UPSA membership is required for athletes entering UPSA-sanctioned Pilipinas Surfing National Tour events. Review the current rules and select the plan that suits your competition schedule.

Two ways to join

Choose your plan

Both plans provide the membership required to compete; access and validity depend on the option selected.

01 / Full seasonBest for multiple events

Annual Membership

One membership covers the Pilipinas Surfing National Tour legs throughout the competition season. It remains valid until the season officially closes.

ValidityOne season
Event accessAll national tour events

PHP 1,000per season

Choose annual membership ↘
02 / Single eventOne competition

One-Time Membership

A single-event membership applies to one event only. It cannot be carried forward to the next event you register for.

ValidityOne event
Event accessOne event only

PHP 500per event
