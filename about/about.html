Who we are

The United Philippine Surfing Association is the national sports association for surfing in the Philippines. Since 2007, UPSA has helped advance the sport and create opportunities for athletes and the wider surfing community.

Its work spans athlete development, competitions, technical standards, and partnerships that help bring Philippine surfing to local and international audiences.
