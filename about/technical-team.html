About UPSA / Competition

Technical
Team

Judges and event officials help ensure competitions are run fairly and consistently.

Competition officials

UPSA’s technical team supports sanctioned events with officials for shortboard and longboard surfing. The association describes its judges as ISA-certified and drawn from regions around the country.
